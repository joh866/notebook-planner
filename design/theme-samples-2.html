Samples 2: more themes

Static pictures, the same Saturday as the first samples page, with look C for the go club and the reading. Section 1 keeps Sleek's shape and swaps in some of the most popular color schemes from code editors and terminals, since Sleek was your favorite. Section 2 shows four different styles that are popular in app design right now. Pick any you'd want as extra choices in Settings. Picking none is fine too.

1. Sleek, in popular color schemes

Each of these is a well-known palette with a light and dark pair (Catppuccin, Rosé Pine, Nord) or a dark-only version. A pair would switch automatically between day and night like Notebook does.

2. Different styles

Glass has frosted, see-through panels over soft color, the look Apple has been pushing. Neubrutalism uses thick black outlines, hard shadows, and loud flat colors. Mono is black and white only, calm and e-ink-like, with no category colors. Both Glass versions are a light and dark pair.
